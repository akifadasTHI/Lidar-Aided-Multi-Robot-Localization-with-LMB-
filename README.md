This repo holds documents of our paper Cooperative LiDAR-Aided Self-Localization of CAVs in Real Urban Scenario

<img width="1902" height="791" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5ed058e8-d8d0-4f50-b090-f5baff3e36eb" />

In urban environments, GNSS-denied regions reduce global vehicle positioning accuracy which in turn negatively impacts the performance of automated driving systems by making high definition (HD) maps and advanced solutions unusable.
Enhanced vehicle positioning is achieved by exploiting collaborative environmental perception based on vision exchange between vehicles. Two benefits are achieved within this method that are highly accurate positioning in canyons and enlightenment of unseen regions on streets.

Average improvement of localization accuracy on two cars over some period is of 67% with respect to ego-GNSS solutions.

Vehicle with lower sensor accuracy is subjected to improvement of 2.4m at 95% confidence level.

This paper proposes a ready-to-implement solution thanks to assumption-free end-to-end structure and the current ADAS technology in automobiles. 

Our test campaigns, with diversity of different positioning solutions (SBAS, SPP) and resolution of LiDARs, point out that the proposed method enhances location of both vehicles.
